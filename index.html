Who We Are
A Kinder, More Equal
Society — Our Mission

Founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation began with a simple belief: compassion and collective action can transform lives. Today, we operate across 28 states, powered by hundreds of dedicated volunteers and a growing community of Amigos.

We are a Section 8 registered non-profit, licensed by the Central Government of India. Our financial transparency is backed by 80G & 12A certifications, making every donation tax-exempt. We are also CSR-1 registered and listed with NITI Aayog, enabling us to partner with corporates and align with national development goals.

From food distribution and education to women's empowerment and environmental conservation, InAmigos addresses root causes — not just symptoms — of inequality and deprivation.

Our Initiatives
Six Projects, One Vision

Each project targets a specific social challenge — together they form a holistic ecosystem of change covering food, education, livelihoods, nature, animals, and skills.

🍱
Food & Clothing

Project SEVA

Distributing meals and clothing to the homeless and underprivileged. No one should sleep hungry or feel the cold without cover. SEVA volunteers spread warmth and comfort, one act of kindness at a time.

🌿 Thousands of meals distributed across India
📚
Education

Project Bachpanshala

Ensuring every child, regardless of poverty, has access to quality education. Bachpanshala creates learning spaces where young minds are nurtured with knowledge, creativity, and hope for a brighter future.

📖 Empowering underprivileged children with education
🐾
Animal Welfare

Project JEEV

Extending compassion to our voiceless friends. JEEV volunteers rescue, feed, and protect stray and wild animals — because a truly humane society cares for all living beings, not just humans.

🐕 Animals rescued, fed, and protected across states
🦋
Women Empowerment

Project UDAAN

Empowering women through skill development and pathways to financial independence. UDAAN helps women rise above societal barriers, discover their potential, and soar towards self-reliance and dignity.

👩 Uplifting women through skills & livelihood creation
🌱
Environment

Project PRAKRITI

Healing our planet through tree plantation drives, environmental awareness, and sustainability efforts. Prakriti volunteers plant saplings with love — nurturing Mother Earth for generations to come.

🌳 Trees planted across drives in multiple cities
🚀
Skill Development

Project VIKAS

Building the workforce of tomorrow. VIKAS has trained over 30,000 interns, equipping young Indians with digital and professional skills that enhance employability and open doors to opportunity.

💼 30,000+ interns trained across India
